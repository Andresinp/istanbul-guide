🍳 2Doğacıyız Gourmet

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)● Classic₺
Kılıçali Paşa, Akarsu Yokuşu Sk. No:41A, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul
💶 ₺ · ₺725 (set breakfast) plus drinks; à la carte menemen ₺220–300 pp (research)
🍽️ plate ₺220–395 (menemen/gözleme/omelettes) source ↗

💬 Eastern cuisine, more touristy.

A Hatay-flavoured breakfast house in Cihangir, different from the usual Turkish kahvaltı and more geared to visitors. It is known for the serpme Antakya breakfast (biberli ekmek, tuzlu yoğurt, zahter, muhammara, sürk), muhlama and Hatay-style gözleme. By its own account it has been running for about 14 years, and Yemek.com says its produce comes from its own land and is sold by the jar.

🍳 3Ağa Bilardo Cafe (the menemen billiard hall)

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)also 🪴 cafés● Alternative
Firuzağa, Taktaki Yokuşu No:2/A (off Sıraselviler Cd.), Cihangir, 34425 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 The best menemen, in a weird place: a billiard hall in Cihangir.

💬 Perfect for two: share a menemen with cheese and a gözleme with cheese, with tea.

An old-school Cihangir billiard hall and kahvehane where locals play billiards, okey and tavla over cheap, always-fresh tea. Reviewers praise the tost, served with pickles on the side. One reviewer called it perhaps the only billiard hall you can come to with family.

🍳 4Van Kahvaltı Evi

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)● Classic
Kılıçali Paşa / Firuzağa, Defterdar Yokuşu No:52/A, 34425 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Favourite #2. Simple, less sophisticated, but everything you get is delicious.

A hugely popular Cihangir institution serving Van-style (eastern Anatolian) breakfast: murtuğa, kavut, otlu peynir, çemen, pişi, gözleme and fresh pomegranate juice. It is famous for its weekend queue; weekdays are calmer. There is a sister branch in Nişantaşı (Valikonağı Cad., Sezai Selek Sk. 19).

🍳 5Kahve 6

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)also 🪴 cafés● Trendy
Kılıçali Paşa Mah., Akarsu Cad., Anahtar Sk. No:13/A, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Favourite #1. Gourmet, curated ingredients, back garden.

💬 Try the İzmir breakfast, the goat cheese omelette and the lor peynirli gözleme.

A cosy, long-standing Cihangir breakfast café with a green back garden with a pool, serving breakfast and home cooking. Highlights include the 'Dolu Dolu' breakfast, the İzmir breakfast (sahanda Bergama tulum, Tire karadut, lor), the aubergine börek, the lor gözleme and the house granola. Time Out was already reviewing it in 2011.

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)also 🪴 cafés● Trendy
Caferağa Mah., Ruşen Ağa Sk. No:3/C, Moda, 34710 Kadıköy/İstanbul

💬 The tastiest eggs Benedict (Moda).

A small neighbourhood breakfast and brunch café in Moda, with eggs Benedict variants on the menu, including salmon Benedict. Time Out reviewed it in 2015, describing breakfast plates with three cheeses, olives, buffalo cream and homemade jam, filling toasts, and eggs and greens from the owners' garden in Yakacık.

🍽️ 12Limonlu Bahçe

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍳 breakfastalso 🪴 cafésalso 🍸 bars● Trendy₺₺
Tomtom Mh., Yeni Çarşı Cd. No:74, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul
💶 ₺₺ · ₺900–1,500 (a main ₺660–1,995 plus a drink) pp (research)
🍸 cocktail ₺650–760 (most cocktails) · 🍷 wine ₺470 (house Ancyra by the glass) · ☕ coffee ₺175 Turkish coffee / ₺260 latte source ↗

💬 Turkish restaurant with very, very nice vibes in Cihangir, set in a garden that makes you forget the hustle and bustle of the city. My go-to place to bring people while it's still warm enough.

A leafy hidden garden for breakfast, a long lunch or evening drinks, more about the setting than the food. The 350 m² garden of lemon, magnolia, pine and linden trees, with resident tortoises, sits behind the French Consulate on Yeni Çarşı, and the Turkish–Mediterranean menu runs from breakfast to late drinks, with house lemonade and cocktails. It opened in June 2001, billed as Istanbul's first 'chill-out zone'.

💬 Delicious food in a plant-filled space. Lots of freelancers work from here, especially on weekdays.

A laid-back, two-storey all-day café-restaurant on Akarsu Caddesi, with a work-friendly upstairs mezzanine whose communal table is popular with laptop workers. The food is Turkish-inspired with a European twist (such as honey-roasted lamb shank), plus brunch and cocktails; good, if a bit pricey. It has been a neighbourhood staple since at least 2010.

🪴 15Emily's Garden

🪴 Cafés to work & hang outalso 🍳 breakfastalso 🍸 bars● Trendy
Pürtelaş Hasan Efendi Mah., Susam Sk. No:13, 34427 Cihangir, Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 A gorgeous garden in the middle of Cihangir. Quite fancy at night, but in the daytime it's quiet and peaceful.

A hidden garden bistro on Susam Sokak: a calm daytime spot for work or brunch, and romantic dinners and drinks at night. A small café front leads to a bar, long tables and a tree-lit back garden, and the Mediterranean/Italian-leaning menu covers pasta, seafood, breakfast and brunch. It occupies the site of The White Mill, which moved to Akaretler, and has been around since at least 2019.

A Cihangir institution with a winter garden: a café serving breakfast and brunch by day, and a bar and restaurant with cocktails and gastropub food at night, with a weekly Wednesday record night. It is an offshoot of the old Kaktüs off İstiklal, opened because many of its regulars had moved to Cihangir, and is one of the neighbourhood's most rooted venues.

🌍 International foodalso 🍳 breakfast● Alternative
Hacımimi Mah., Lüleci Hendek Cd. No:23A, 34425 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Gorgeous Syrian/Lebanese restaurant in Tophane. Go for the huge Syrian breakfast: similar to the Turkish style, with more delicious dips.

A very popular all-day Lebanese-Syrian café-restaurant between Galata and Tophane; go for the huge Middle-Eastern breakfast, which adds hummus, fatteh, labneh with za'atar and falafel to the Turkish kahvaltı. It also serves mezes and grills all day. A pricier sister venue, Arada Endülüs, is about 60 m away at Kemeraltı Cd. 31.

A smart restaurant with a Bosphorus-view terrace in the Sakıp Sabancı Museum, run as the training restaurant of Mutfak Sanatları Akademisi (MSA, Istanbul Culinary Arts Academy): students cook and serve under professional chefs. The food is strong, mixing world cuisines with seasonal Turkish produce, though service can be patchy. It opened around 2017 in the space formerly occupied by Müzedechanga and is open to the public without a museum ticket.
