🌇 51Okra İstanbul

🌇 Rooftops & sunsetalso ✨ fancyalso 🍸 bars● Posh₺₺₺
Gümüşsuyu, Osmanlı Sk. No:1/B, 34437 Beyoğlu/İstanbul (The Marmara Taksim, 20th floor)

An upscale restaurant with a bar on the 20th floor of The Marmara Taksim, with floor-to-ceiling views from the Bosphorus to the historic peninsula; go for a sunset cocktail at the bar or a full dinner. It opened around 2023 with a seasonal, open-fire Mediterranean/Turkish menu, cocktails and Turkish wines, and the bar section was added later. It is listed in the Michelin Guide (₺₺₺).

🌇 52Büyük Londra Hotel terrace bar

🌇 Rooftops & sunsetalso 🍸 bars● Classic
Asmalı Mescit Mh, Meşrutiyet Cd. No:53, 34430 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Chill and not so expensive. Go at sunset, in time for the call to prayer echoing over the mosque-filled skyline.

A low-key, not-too-expensive seasonal hotel rooftop for a sunset drink over the Golden Horn and the old city, with a simple menu. It belongs to the Büyük Londra, an 1892 hotel built for Orient Express passengers and known for its eccentric antique lobby.

⚠ The terrace bar is reported to open in the warm season only; it may be closed from late October. Check before going.

🌇 53Mikla bar

🌇 Rooftops & sunsetalso ✨ fancyalso 🍸 bars● Posh
Asmalı Mescit, The Marmara Pera, Meşrutiyet Cd. No:15, 34430 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 The bar of this exclusive restaurant is open to everyone without a reservation. Probably the best view of Istanbul.

💬 A small terrace in winter, still really worth it; in summer the top floor becomes a whole bar. A bit fancier than the others.

A fancy roof-terrace bar on top of The Marmara Pera, with panoramic views over the Golden Horn, the old city and the Bosphorus, and cocktails priced accordingly; it is open until 02:00. It belongs to Mikla, chef Mehmet Gürs's New Anatolian restaurant, listed among the World's 50 Best Restaurants (2022).

🌇 54Balkon

🌇 Rooftops & sunsetalso 🍸 bars● Classic₺₺
Asmalı Mescit, Şehbender Sk. No:5 D:M (6th floor), 34430 Beyoğlu/İstanbul
💶 ₺₺
🍸 cocktail ₺735–860 · 🍷 wine ₺440–500 · ☕ coffee ₺150–200 source ↗

A casual, mid-priced rooftop restaurant-bar on the 6th floor of a building on Şehbender Sokak, with a big view over Tünel, the Golden Horn and the historic peninsula, easier on the wallet than the hotel rooftops nearby. It serves pub-style food (burgers, pizza, salads) and a long cocktail and wine list, and the evening programme moves to dance music. It has been running since 2004.

⚠ Check the bill: a July 2026 Yandex review says staff once tried to add a ₺300 service charge and removed it only after the guest objected.

🌇 55Monkey Istanbul

🌇 Rooftops & sunsetalso 🍸 barsalso 🪩 clubs● Trendy
Nejat Eczacıbaşı Binası (İKSV), Sadi Konuralp Cd. No:5, 7th floor, Şişhane, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 More electronic music, leaning towards techno.

A sunset-to-late cocktail rooftop on the 7th floor of the İKSV (Istanbul Foundation for Culture and Arts) building in Şişhane, with a wooden terrace over the Golden Horn and the historic peninsula. It serves signature cocktails and snacks while resident and guest DJs play laid-back house and disco (Time Out).

Asian side: Kadıköy & Moda

🌇 113Moda Çay Bahçesi

🌇 Rooftops & sunsetalso ☀️ all-day● Classic
Caferağa, Park İçi Yolu, 34710 Kadıköy/İstanbul

The big family tea garden (Moda Aile Çay Bahçesi) next to Moda Park, on the hill above the Sea of Marmara, under large shady trees. Tea, oralet and ayran at low prices, with views across to the old city: a classic sunset spot. Busy on weekends; keep walking to find a table by the water.

🪩 57Şahika

🪩 Clubs / electronic musicalso 🍸 barsalso 🌇 rooftops● Alternative
Hüseyinağa Mah., Nevizade Sk. No:5, Beyoğlu/İstanbul

A busy multi-storey Nevizade landmark: a pub at street level and lounge floors for drinks, and a rooftop bar/club with Golden Horn views hosting techno, bass and D&B parties late in the week, as well as concerts, exhibitions and panels. Küçük Meyhane has moved into the building.
