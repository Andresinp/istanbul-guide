Cihangir

🍽️ 11Tomtom Kebap

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🥙 street food● Classic₺
Firuzağa, Yeni Çarşı Cd. 39 A, 34425 Beyoğlu/İstanbul
💶 ₺ · ~₺600 pp (research)

A tiny kebab and dürüm shop on Yeni Çarşı Caddesi, very popular with tourists and often with a queue. It is known for Adana and Urfa kebab, lamb dürüm, hummus and lentil soup, with a few sidewalk tables. It is said to have been founded in 2018.

🍽️ 12Limonlu Bahçe

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍳 breakfastalso ☀️ all-dayalso 🍸 bars● Trendy₺₺
Tomtom Mh., Yeni Çarşı Cd. No:74, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul
💶 ₺₺ · ₺900–1,500 (a main ₺660–1,995 plus a drink) pp (research)
🍸 cocktail ₺650–760 (most cocktails) · 🍷 wine ₺470 (house Ancyra by the glass) · ☕ coffee ₺175 Turkish coffee / ₺260 latte source ↗

💬 Turkish restaurant with very, very nice vibes in Cihangir, set in a garden that makes you forget the hustle and bustle of the city. My go-to place to bring people while it's still warm enough.

A leafy hidden garden for breakfast, a long lunch or evening drinks, more about the setting than the food. The 350 m² garden of lemon, magnolia, pine and linden trees, with resident tortoises, sits behind the French Consulate on Yeni Çarşı, and the Turkish–Mediterranean menu runs from breakfast to late drinks, with house lemonade and cocktails. It opened in June 2001, billed as Istanbul's first 'chill-out zone'.

Beyoğlu, Taksim & Galata

🍽️ 38Zübeyir Ocakbaşı

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurants● Classic
Şehit Muhtar Mh., Bekar Sk. No:28, 34435 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

A classic, busy ocakbaşı over three floors in Taksim; sit at the grill counter and book ahead at weekends. It is known for Adana kebab, ribs (kaburga), liver, gavurdağı salad and quince dessert. Grill master Zübeyir opened it in 2006, and the family's start in the trade goes back to 1986 with brothers Hamit and Zübeyir.

🍽️ 39Fıccın

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍲 lokantas● Classic
Asmalı Mescit Mh., Kallavi Sk. No:13/1 (off İstiklal Cd.), 34430 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Outdoor restaurant just off İstiklal, on a little side street. Delicious Caucasian food with tasty wines.

💬 Must-tries: the shredded Circassian chicken (çerkez tavuğu), the dumplings, and the meze starters, including aubergine in tomato sauce and beetroot with yoghurt.

Long-running, good-value Circassian home cooking on pedestrian Kallavi Sokak off İstiklal, with outdoor tables. Named after its signature Circassian meat pastry, it also serves çerkez tavuğu, Circassian mantı and olive-oil dishes, and unusually for a lokanta it serves alcohol. Leyla Kılıç Karakaynak opened it in 1996, and it has since spread across several shopfronts on the street.

🍽️ 40Eleos

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurants● Classic
Şahkulu Mh., İstiklal Cd. No:231, Hıdivyal Palas Kat 2, Beyoğlu (Tünel)/İstanbul

💬 A tasty rakı-balık (rakı and fish) on İstiklal. Not the cheapest, but some of the best fish I've ever eaten, and a stunning view onto the Bosphorus.

A Greek-leaning fish and meze restaurant for rakı-balık with a view, on the 2nd floor of the historic Hıdivyal Palas on İstiklal near Tünel, with blue-and-white decor and a terrace. It is known for fresh fish, grilled octopus and herb-heavy Turkish and Greek mezes. It is pricey for fish, which is charged at the daily market price. There is a separate, older branch in Yeşilköy.

🍽️ 41Tavanarası

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurants● Classic
Asmalı Mescit Cd. No:10 Kat 6 (Emre Han), 34430 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

A cosy, cramped attic restaurant on the 6th floor of Emre Han off Asmalı Mescit, reached by a rickety lift, with wooden decor and tightly packed tables. It is popular with locals; you write your order on paper. It is known for güveç (casseroles), meze, mücver and grilled fish, and serves alcohol.

Karaköy & Tophane

🍽️ 61Karaköy Lokantası

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍲 lokantas● Classic₺₺
Kemankeş Mh., Kemankeş Cd. No:57, 34425 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

A smart, good-value harbourside restaurant with a tiled interior that works as a lokanta at lunch and a meze-and-rakı spot in the evening. It serves Turkish and Mediterranean meze, shared plates and traditional dishes with subtle Greek influences; the taramasalata and beef börek are highlights. It is listed in the Michelin Guide (₺₺).

🍽️ 62Mahkeme Lokantası

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍲 lokantas● Classic
Azapkapı, Perşembe Pazarı Cd. No:14 (Yoğurtçu Han), 34421 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

A tradesmen's lunch lokanta by day that becomes a lively classic meyhane at night, with crowds spilling onto the street. It is known for Girit ezme, pastırmalı humus, paçanga böreği and grilled octopus. It is housed in the historic Yoğurtçu Han, with a well-kept brick interior, and is named after the old Genoese courthouse across the street.

Setüstü, Kabataş & Beşiktaş

🍽️ 68Ali Ocakbaşı Gümüşsuyu

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurants● Classic₺₺₺
Gümüşsuyu, İnönü Cd. No:22, 34870 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Kebab and Turkish food, with a Bosphorus view.

An upscale ocakbaşı on İnönü Caddesi with a Bosphorus view and a polished take on kebab and meze: charcoal kebabs, lahmacun, and mezes such as muhammara and Girit ezme. It is the Gümüşsuyu branch of a grill house group with branches in Karaköy, Suadiye, Amsterdam and Barcelona, which promotes zero-waste, sustainable sourcing.

Asian side: Kadıköy & Moda

🍽️ 99Çiya Sofrası

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍲 lokantas● Classic
Caferağa, Güneşlibahçe Sk. No:43, 34710 Kadıköy/İstanbul

💬 Turkish food on the Asian side.

A canteen-style counter of rotating regional dishes in the Kadıköy market; go at lunch for the widest choice. Musa Dağdeviren's restaurant grew out of a modest kebab shop and researches and revives regional Anatolian, Mesopotamian and Caucasian recipes. It was featured in Netflix's Chef's Table (vol. 5) and ranked on Opinionated About Dining's Casual Europe list in 2024 and 2025.

🍽️ 100Viktor Levi Şarap Evi

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍸 bars● Classic
Caferağa Mh., Moda Cd., Damacı Sk. No:4, Kadıköy/İstanbul (behind Reks Cinema)

💬 A beautiful garden to enjoy a long evening dinner with friends (Moda).

A big, lively wine house in Moda with a large courtyard garden for long dinners with friends, where the wine matters more than the food. It pours house wines such as Papaz Karası and Ada Karası and fruit wines, with a bistro menu of grills, pasta and cheese plates. It descends from the historic Viktor Levi wine shop in Beyoğlu, reportedly founded in 1914; the Beyoğlu venue has closed.

🥙 35Dürümzade

🥙 Street food & cheap eatsalso 🍽️ turkish● Classic
Hüseyinağa Mah., Kamer Hatun Cd. No:26/A (corner of Topçekenler Sk.), 34435 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Hole-in-the-wall for Hatay food, spicier than western Turkey's. Famous since Anthony Bourdain gave it his blessing. The best dürüm I've had in Turkey.

A tiny hole-in-the-wall charcoal dürüm shop a few minutes' walk from İstiklal Caddesi, serving lamb, Adana, chicken and liver dürüm. It became famous after Anthony Bourdain ate a lamb şiş dürüm there for the Istanbul episode of 'No Reservations', and has had a lot of tourist traffic since. The double-lamb 'duble kuzu dürüm' is the signature.
