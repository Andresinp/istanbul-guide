🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)

🍳 72Avokado Bar

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)also 🪴 cafésalso ☕ coffee● Trendy₺₺
Teşvikiye, Ahmet Fetgari Sk. No:29/B, 34365 Şişli/İstanbul
💶 ₺₺ · ₺900–1,400 (one dish plus one coffee) pp (research)
🍽️ plate ₺630–1,080 · ☕ coffee ₺275 source ↗

💬 Healthy food: vegan, vegetarian and more.

A healthy all-day café and specialty-coffee bar in Teşvikiye, Nişantaşı, for brunch or lunch: avocado tartines on sourdough, grain bowls (quinoa patties, salmon), smoothies and smoothie bowls, and refined-sugar-free desserts, with many vegan, vegetarian and gluten-free options. Despite the 'bar' name, there is no alcohol on the menu.

✨ Fancy dinners

✨ 73TURK Fatih Tutak

✨ Fancy dinners● Posh₺₺₺
Cumhuriyet Mah., Hacıahmet Silahşör Cad., Yeniyol Sk. No:2, 34440 Şişli/İstanbul (Bomonti)
💶 ₺₺₺ · ₺16,500–19,900 tasting menu (sources disagree; wine pairing ~₺8,900–9,500) pp (research)

Chef Fatih Tutak's tasting-menu restaurant in Bomonti, a top-tier experience lasting about three hours; book weeks ahead. A single menu of about 12–14 courses built on Turkish regional produce is served across a city-view lounge, a dining room facing the kitchen and, for dessert, the open kitchen, with a strong Turkish wine list. It won two MICHELIN stars in the first Istanbul guide (October 2022) and is still listed with two.

🌍 International food

🌍 74Seorabeol Korean Restaurant

🌍 International food● Classic
Raşit Rıza Sk. No:3, Mecidiyeköy, 34847 Şişli/İstanbul

A proper, long-running Korean restaurant in the business district, serving Istanbul's Korean community, and pricier than average. It is known for free banchan refills, yukgaejang and Korean fried chicken.

☕ Specialty coffee

☕ 75Spada Coffee (Nişantaşı)

☕ Specialty coffeealso 🪴 cafés● Trendy
Teşvikiye, Av. Süreyya Ağaoğlu Sk. No:8/E, 34365 Şişli/İstanbul

A small, self-service specialty coffee bar of Spada Roastery on a quiet Teşvikiye side street, with about 10 tables indoors and out. It serves espresso drinks, pour-overs of its own beans, Japanese matcha, house-made cold teas and refined-sugar-free bakes. It has been here since 2016; Spada also has a branch in Fişekhane and a roastery in Bakırköy.

☕ 76Petra Roasting Co. (Topağacı)

☕ Specialty coffeealso 🪴 cafés● Trendy
Teşvikiye, Prof. Dr. Orhan Ersek Sk. 18/7, 34365 Şişli/İstanbul

Petra's sit-down neighbourhood café in historic Topağacı, Nişantaşı, with table service and a bar-food menu as well as coffee. Petra describes it as a real mahalle coffee house with a 1930s-inspired interior. Petra Roasting Co. is one of Istanbul's early specialty roasters, with its HQ and roastery in Gayrettepe.

⚠ OpenStreetMap labels this address 'Kronotrop Topağacı', while Petra's 2026 site still lists the café. Check before going.

🍸 Bars & wine

🍸 77Dekk

🍸 Bars & winealso 🥙 street food● Trendy
Harbiye, Darülbedai Cd. No:4 (Lütfi Kırdar Congress Centre terrace), 34367 Şişli/İstanbul

An open-air 2,500 m² food hall and cocktail bar (dekkBar) on the terrace of the Lütfi Kırdar Congress Centre near Maçka Park, with street-food stalls, concerts and workshops; best in good weather. It opened in autumn 2021, and a second, indoor-outdoor dekk opened in Akmerkez on 11 October 2025.

⚠ Dekk's own site now lists only its Akmerkez location (opened Oct 2025); this Maçka terrace may have closed. Check before going.

🍸 82Bosphorus Brewing Company

🍸 Bars & wine● Classic
Esentepe, Yıldız Posta Cd. No:1 D:1A, 34394 Şişli/İstanbul

An English-Turkish brewpub near Gayrettepe metro in the business district, with a terrace and beer brewed on site; it is one of the few places in Istanbul pouring its own draught craft beer, with Istanbul Pale Ale as the flagship and a reported 12 house taps. The food is British gastropub style (ploughman's, bangers and mash, fish and chips). Its website says it has hosted guests for over 14 years.
