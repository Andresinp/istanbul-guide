How this was made

Everything here is Andrés's: his written Istanbul guide (cafés, breakfasts, street food, restaurants, bars, areas, hamams), his "same but different" table, his Google My Map "Bars-Restaurants- other", and his edits of 8 Oct 2026.

  • Google Maps short links from the guide and the table were resolved to their places. Those places link to the original pins; the others link to a Google Maps search for their exact name and address.
  • Places named without a link were identified on the web; the doubtful ones are marked, and the ones we could not pin are listed below.
  • Every place was checked for open/closed status on 8 Oct 2026, and, where we could read it, its Google rating, per-person price range and the topics reviewers mention.
  • "In practice" lines and item prices (cocktail, beer, wine, plate, coffee) come from reviews and menus, each linked. Prices nobody published are left blank, not guessed. Turkish prices rise fast, so treat them as a rough guide.
  • Coordinates were cross-checked against OpenStreetMap, the My Map pins and Google. Area shading is approximate.
  • Events were researched on 8 Oct 2026. Each carries its source and a confidence flag.
