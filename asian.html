Asian side: Kadıköy & Moda

A ferry ride away: Moda's cafés and wine houses, Kadıköy's market food and sunset beers on the rocks.

🏘️ 95Moda

🏘️ Neighbourhoods● Trendy

💬 On the Asian side; it has recently stolen the crown from Cihangir as the 'trendiest' place in Istanbul. You can get anything here, including Portuguese custard tarts, Japanese mochi and Vietnamese pho.

💬 Best part: hanging out on the rocks along the waterfront for a sunset beer. Getting there means taking the ferry across the Bosphorus, another key part of Istanbul.

A trendy neighbourhood on a small peninsula in Kadıköy on the Asian side, a short walk from Kadıköy ferry pier. It is known for its cafés and bars, the Moda Sahili seafront park, and the rocky shoreline where people gather at sunset.

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)

🍳 96700 Gram

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)also 🪴 cafés● Trendy
Caferağa Mah., Ruşen Ağa Sk. No:3/C, Moda, 34710 Kadıköy/İstanbul

💬 The tastiest eggs Benedict (Moda).

A small neighbourhood breakfast and brunch café in Moda, with eggs Benedict variants on the menu, including salmon Benedict. Time Out reviewed it in 2015, describing breakfast plates with three cheeses, olives, buffalo cream and homemade jam, filling toasts, and eggs and greens from the owners' garden in Yakacık.

🍽️ 97Çiya Sofrası

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍲 lokantas● Classic
Caferağa, Güneşlibahçe Sk. No:43, 34710 Kadıköy/İstanbul

💬 Turkish food on the Asian side.

A canteen-style counter of rotating regional dishes in the Kadıköy market; go at lunch for the widest choice. Musa Dağdeviren's restaurant grew out of a modest kebab shop and researches and revives regional Anatolian, Mesopotamian and Caucasian recipes. It was featured in Netflix's Chef's Table (vol. 5) and ranked on Opinionated About Dining's Casual Europe list in 2024 and 2025.

🍽️ 98Viktor Levi Şarap Evi

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍸 bars● Classic
Caferağa Mh., Moda Cd., Damacı Sk. No:4, Kadıköy/İstanbul (behind Reks Cinema)

💬 A beautiful garden to enjoy a long evening dinner with friends (Moda).

A big, lively wine house in Moda with a large courtyard garden for long dinners with friends, where the wine matters more than the food. It pours house wines such as Papaz Karası and Ada Karası and fruit wines, with a bistro menu of grills, pasta and cheese plates. It descends from the historic Viktor Levi wine shop in Beyoğlu, reportedly founded in 1914; the Beyoğlu venue has closed.

☕ 100Montag Coffee (Moda)

☕ Specialty coffeealso 🪴 cafés● Trendy
Caferağa Mah. Bademaltı Sok. No:22/A, Moda, Kadıköy/İstanbul

The Moda branch of Montag Coffee, a Kadıköy specialty roaster, on a back street steps from Story. Montag roasts its own beans, has cafés in Kadıköy (Muvakkithane Cad.), Moda and Bomonti, and is credited with bringing third-wave coffee to Kadıköy in the mid-2010s (2014 or 2015; sources differ).
