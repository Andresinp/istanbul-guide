Wine house descended from the historic Viktor Levi wine shop founded in Beyoğlu (1914, per aggregator; the Beyoğlu venue has closed per Time Out). It now operates in Moda with a large courtyard garden, house wines such as Papaz Karası and Ada Karası, fruit wines and a bistro menu of grills, pasta and cheese plates.
