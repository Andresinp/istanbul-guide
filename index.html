Good to know

🧻 Toilet techTurkish toilets usually have a built-in bidet spout (tuvalet duşu), opened with the little valve on the wall to the right. It's for washing, not a leak.
🤧 Nose etiquetteBlowing your nose loudly in public, especially at the table, is frowned upon. Excuse yourself and keep it discreet.
🍳 BreakfastTurkish breakfast is a feast: cheeses, olives, tomatoes, cucumbers, jams, honey, eggs. It's taken seriously and served all day. Go with a group: the more of you, the more you can taste.
🫖 TeaTea comes in small tulip-shaped glasses and offering it is offering friendship. Say yes.
⛴️ FerriesCross to the Asian side by ferry (Karaköy or Kabataş to Kadıköy): it's part of the city, not just transport.
