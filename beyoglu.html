Beyoğlu, Taksim & Galata

İstiklal and its side streets: Asmalımescit meyhanes, rooftop bars over the Golden Horn, Galata Tower and Şişhane's clubs.

🏘️ Neighbourhoods

🏘️ 30Galata

🏘️ Neighbourhoods● Must-see

💬 Right next to Cihangir, only 15 min walk away. It has the famous Galata Tower (definitely worth going up for the incredible view), as well as a bunch of tasty restaurants, cafés and vintage shops.

A touristy but atmospheric hill around Galata Tower, good for wandering, food and shops. The historic Genoese-era quarter lies on the north side of the Golden Horn, centred on the 14th-century tower, with steep lanes of restaurants, cafés, music and vintage shops running down from Tünel and İstiklal towards Karaköy.

🥙 Street food & cheap eats

🥙 32Suat Usta Mersin Tantuni

🥙 Street food & cheap eats● Classic
Katip Mustafa Çelebi Mah., Büyükparmakkapı Tel Sk. No:1 (Çukurlu Çeşme Sk. corner), 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

A long-running, late-night Mersin tantuni counter in the back streets off İstiklal near Taksim, and the obvious replacement for the closed Emine Ana. It is a one-dish shop serving tantuni dürüm with Mersin şalgam and pickled peppers, where crowds from İstiklal and Nevizade end their night.

🥙 33Anas Chicken

🥙 Street food & cheap eatsalso 🌍 international● Classic
Kuloğlu Mah., Sadri Alışık Sk. No:8, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 A chain with restaurants mostly in Syria and a few in Turkey; Syrian friends are obsessed with it. Get the fried chicken or a dürüm to go for an easy lunch.

Syrian fried chicken and shawarma, mainly takeaway: broasted chicken, chicken shawarma dürüm and kabsa. The chain (Anas Gıda Grubu) was founded in Damascus in 1991 and lists three active Istanbul branches: Beyoğlu (Taksim), a short walk from İstiklal, plus Aksaray and Kayaşehir.

🥙 34Dürümzade

🥙 Street food & cheap eatsalso 🍽️ turkish● Classic
Hüseyinağa Mah., Kamer Hatun Cd. No:26/A (corner of Topçekenler Sk.), 34435 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Hole-in-the-wall for Hatay food, spicier than western Turkey's. Famous since Anthony Bourdain gave it his blessing. The best dürüm I've had in Turkey.

A tiny hole-in-the-wall charcoal dürüm shop a few minutes' walk from İstiklal Caddesi, serving lamb, Adana, chicken and liver dürüm. It became famous after Anthony Bourdain ate a lamb şiş dürüm there for the Istanbul episode of 'No Reservations', and has had a lot of tourist traffic since. The double-lamb 'duble kuzu dürüm' is the signature.

🍲 Lokantas: home cooking

🍲 36Helvetia

🍲 Lokantas: home cooking● Classic
Asmalı Mescit, General Yazgan Sk. No:8, 34430 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

A cheap, busy, no-frills lokanta by Tünel where you point to choose from seasonal, mostly vegetarian home-cooked dishes on the counter by the open kitchen, such as pazı borani, köfte, dolma and legumes; lunch is the peak, and there is no alcohol. It occupies a former beerhouse, and the old 'Helvetia' name painted on the wall is a remnant of the original building. Time Out once gave it its 'best home-cooked food' award.

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurants

🍽️ 37Zübeyir Ocakbaşı

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurants● Classic
Şehit Muhtar Mh., Bekar Sk. No:28, 34435 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

A classic, busy ocakbaşı over three floors in Taksim; sit at the grill counter and book ahead at weekends. It is known for Adana kebab, ribs (kaburga), liver, gavurdağı salad and quince dessert. Grill master Zübeyir opened it in 2006, and the family's start in the trade goes back to 1986 with brothers Hamit and Zübeyir.

🍽️ 38Fıccın

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍲 lokantas● Classic
Asmalı Mescit Mh., Kallavi Sk. No:13/1 (off İstiklal Cd.), 34430 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Outdoor restaurant just off İstiklal, on a little side street. Delicious Caucasian food with tasty wines.

💬 Must-tries: the shredded Circassian chicken (çerkez tavuğu), the dumplings, and the meze starters, including aubergine in tomato sauce and beetroot with yoghurt.

Long-running, good-value Circassian home cooking on pedestrian Kallavi Sokak off İstiklal, with outdoor tables. Named after its signature Circassian meat pastry, it also serves çerkez tavuğu, Circassian mantı and olive-oil dishes, and unusually for a lokanta it serves alcohol. Leyla Kılıç Karakaynak opened it in 1996, and it has since spread across several shopfronts on the street.

🍽️ 39Eleos

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurants● Classic
Şahkulu Mh., İstiklal Cd. No:231, Hıdivyal Palas Kat 2, Beyoğlu (Tünel)/İstanbul

💬 A tasty rakı-balık (rakı and fish) on İstiklal. Not the cheapest, but some of the best fish I've ever eaten, and a stunning view onto the Bosphorus.

A Greek-leaning fish and meze restaurant for rakı-balık with a view, on the 2nd floor of the historic Hıdivyal Palas on İstiklal near Tünel, with blue-and-white decor and a terrace. It is known for fresh fish, grilled octopus and herb-heavy Turkish and Greek mezes. It is pricey for fish, which is charged at the daily market price. There is a separate, older branch in Yeşilköy.

🍽️ 40Tavanarası

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurants● Classic
Asmalı Mescit Cd. No:10 Kat 6 (Emre Han), 34430 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

A cosy, cramped attic restaurant on the 6th floor of Emre Han off Asmalı Mescit, reached by a rickety lift, with wooden decor and tightly packed tables. It is popular with locals; you write your order on paper. It is known for güveç (casseroles), meze, mücver and grilled fish, and serves alcohol.

✨ 42Aheste

✨ Fancy dinnersalso 🍸 bars● Posh₺₺₺
Asmalı Mescit Mah., Meşrutiyet Cad. No:107/F, 34430 Beyoğlu/İstanbul
💶 ₺₺₺ · ₺4,800 tasting menu (+₺3,200 for 5-glass wine pairing) pp (research)

💬 Slightly more upmarket: a delicious modern take on traditional Turkish food. You sit in a cosy candlelit alcove in the historic Pera district. Service is top notch and the wine list is fabulous.

💬 My friend James says this is the best restaurant in Istanbul.

An intimate modern-Turkish meze and tasting-menu restaurant in Pera, upmarket but not stuffy, with a bistro area and a vaulted, candlelit dining room. The menu mixes traditional dishes (grilled lamb heart, kokoreç, dudi pilavı) with modern mezes, with good vegetarian options. It is listed in the MICHELIN Guide Istanbul.

🌍 International food

🌍 43Galaktion

🌍 International food● Trendy₺₺
Asmalı Mescit Mah., Şehbender Sk., 34430 Beyoğlu/İstanbul
💶 ₺₺
🍺 beer ₺350–400 (500 ml) · ☕ coffee ₺195–220 source ↗

💬 Georgian, very traditional. Everything is delicious; the standard order for two is the Georgian salad, beef khinkali, Adjaruli khachapuri and chakhokhbili (tomato chicken stew).

💬 It is *compulsory* to wash it down with a shot of chacha, a very strong Georgian spirit.

A homely Georgian kitchen with generous portions of khinkali, adjaruli khachapuri, chakhokhbili and other stews, plus Georgian wines, served in a book-filled room on Şehbender Sokak in Asmalımescit. Founded by Irakli Kakabadze and named after the poet Galaktion Tabidze, it began in a small storefront in Galata and moved near Taksim before settling here.

🍸 45Hazzo Pulo (Passage Hazzopulo)

🍸 Bars & winealso 🏛️ sights● Classic
Evliya Çelebi Mah., Meşrutiyet Cd. No:31B (Hazzopulo Pasajı), Beyoğlu/İstanbul

Hazzopulo Pasajı is a 19th-century passage between İstiklal and Meşrutiyet with a courtyard of tea houses and cafés. The wine stop inside is Hazzo Pulo (Meşrutiyet Cd. 31B), a small wine house and restaurant with a list of Turkish wines, which gives the passage its name in guidebooks.

🌇 49Büyük Londra Hotel terrace bar

🌇 Rooftops & sunsetalso 🍸 bars● Classic
Asmalı Mescit Mh, Meşrutiyet Cd. No:53, 34430 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Chill and not so expensive. Go at sunset, in time for the call to prayer echoing over the mosque-filled skyline.

A low-key, not-too-expensive seasonal hotel rooftop for a sunset drink over the Golden Horn and the old city, with a simple menu. It belongs to the Büyük Londra, an 1892 hotel built for Orient Express passengers and known for its eccentric antique lobby.

🏛️ Sights, museums & walks

🏛️ 56Galata Tower

🏛️ Sights, museums & walks● Must-see
Bereketzade Mah., Galata Kulesi, Beyoğlu, İstanbul

💬 Definitely worth going up for the incredible view.

An iconic medieval stone tower with a 360° viewing balcony over the Golden Horn and the Bosphorus; expect queues. The Genoese built it in the 14th century as part of the Galata walls, and since its 2020 restoration it has been run as a museum by the Ministry of Culture and Tourism.
