Karaköy & Tophane

Down by the water: ivy-covered lanes, hamams, fish wraps, soup houses and galleries.

🏘️ Neighbourhoods

🏘️ 60Karaköy

🏘️ Neighbourhoods● Classic

💬 10 min walk from Cihangir, down the hill by the water. A few ivy-filled gorgeous streets to wander around. Grab a drink at any bar that catches your eye.

A waterfront district of cafés and bars in restored warehouses and ivy-covered lanes, at the Beyoğlu end of the Galata Bridge. The old port and banking district has filled with cafés, bars and galleries since the 2010s, and ferries leave from Karaköy pier.

🥙 Street food & cheap eats

🥙 61Karaköy Çorba Evi

🥙 Street food & cheap eatsalso 🍲 lokantas● Classic
Kemankeş Karamustafa Paşa Mah., Mumhane Cd. No:35/A, 34425 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Soups. You can ask for a ½ portion as a first course, then go on to the fish restaurant.

A cheap soup house on Mumhane Caddesi with about 20 soups, from mercimek and ezogelin to işkembe and kelle paça; order a half portion as a starter before a fish dinner. It started in 2011 as a soup-and-stew place and has traded as Karaköy Çorba Evi since 2014. Its website also mentions a Kadıköy location.

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurants

🍽️ 63Karaköy Lokantası

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍲 lokantas● Classic₺₺
Kemankeş Mh., Kemankeş Cd. No:57, 34425 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

A smart, good-value harbourside restaurant with a tiled interior that works as a lokanta at lunch and a meze-and-rakı spot in the evening. It serves Turkish and Mediterranean meze, shared plates and traditional dishes with subtle Greek influences; the taramasalata and beef börek are highlights. It is listed in the Michelin Guide (₺₺).

🍽️ 64Mahkeme Lokantası

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍲 lokantas● Classic
Azapkapı, Perşembe Pazarı Cd. No:14 (Yoğurtçu Han), 34421 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

A tradesmen's lunch lokanta by day that becomes a lively classic meyhane at night, with crowds spilling onto the street. It is known for Girit ezme, pastırmalı humus, paçanga böreği and grilled octopus. It is housed in the historic Yoğurtçu Han, with a well-kept brick interior, and is named after the old Genoese courthouse across the street.

🌍 International food

🌍 65Arada Café

🌍 International foodalso 🍳 breakfast● Alternative
Hacımimi Mah., Lüleci Hendek Cd. No:23A, 34425 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Gorgeous Syrian/Lebanese restaurant in Tophane. Go for the huge Syrian breakfast: similar to the Turkish style, with more delicious dips.

A very popular all-day Lebanese-Syrian café-restaurant between Galata and Tophane; go for the huge Middle-Eastern breakfast, which adds hummus, fatteh, labneh with za'atar and falafel to the Turkish kahvaltı. It also serves mezes and grills all day. A pricier sister venue, Arada Endülüs, is about 60 m away at Kemeraltı Cd. 31.

🍸 Bars & wine

🍸 66Karaköy Gümrük

🍸 Bars & wine● Trendy
Kemankeş Karamustafa Paşa, Gümrük Sk. No:4, 34425 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

An all-day restaurant in Karaköy's former customs-directorate building that turns into a cosy bar in the evening, with a modern-Turkish/Mediterranean kitchen, rakı, Turkish wine and cocktails. Reviewers like the interior, the dumplings and the cocktails; the upstairs bar has dark tones and black-and-white photos for a vintage feel. The building was reportedly restored and opened as Karaköy Gümrük in 2014.

🛁 Hamams & treat yourself

🛁 68Kılıç Ali Paşa Hamamı

🛁 Hamams & treat yourself● Must-see₺₺₺
Kemankeş Mah., Hamam Sk. No:1, 34425 Tophane, Karaköy, Beyoğlu/İstanbul
💶 ₺₺₺ · ₺4,700–9,700 (ritual alone to ritual + 50-min massage) pp (research)

💬 The more expensive but stunning option, in Karaköy.

A high-end, strictly single-sex hamam: women 08:00–16:00, men in the evening. The Traditional Hamam Ritual (₺4,700) means resting on the heated marble platform, a kese scrub by an attendant and a foam wash at the basins; massages are extra (50-minute full body ₺5,000, 25-minute partial ₺3,800). Mimar Sinan built it in 1578–1583 for admiral Kılıç Ali Pasha to serve the Tophane mariners, its 14 m dome is the second largest of any Istanbul hamam, and it reopened after a long restoration.
