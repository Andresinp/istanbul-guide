🏘️ 1Cihangir

🏘️ Neighbourhoods● Alternative

💬 Most of the places in this guide are in Cihangir or the wider Beyoğlu area.

A leafy, café-heavy residential quarter and the base for most places in this guide. It sits on a hillside in Beyoğlu between Taksim/Sıraselviler and the Bosphorus shore at Fındıklı, and is known for its bohemian, artsy reputation, its cafés and its street cats. Cihangir Mosque stands at its lower end.

🏘️ 32Galata

🏘️ Neighbourhoods● Must-see

💬 Right next to Cihangir, only 15 min walk away. It has the famous Galata Tower (definitely worth going up for the incredible view), as well as a bunch of tasty restaurants, cafés and vintage shops.

A touristy but atmospheric hill around Galata Tower, good for wandering, food and shops. The historic Genoese-era quarter lies on the north side of the Golden Horn, centred on the 14th-century tower, with steep lanes of restaurants, cafés, music and vintage shops running down from Tünel and İstiklal towards Karaköy.

🏘️ 60Karaköy

🏘️ Neighbourhoods● Classic

💬 10 min walk from Cihangir, down the hill by the water. A few ivy-filled gorgeous streets to wander around. Grab a drink at any bar that catches your eye.

A waterfront district of cafés and bars in restored warehouses and ivy-covered lanes, at the Beyoğlu end of the Galata Bridge. The old port and banking district has filled with cafés, bars and galleries since the 2010s, and ferries leave from Karaköy pier.

A lively, local, football-mad district on the European side of the Bosphorus, home of Beşiktaş JK and its Tüpraş Stadium; busy on match days. The çarşı (market area) behind the ferry pier is packed with cheap pubs, meyhanes and student bars.
