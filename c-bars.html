Cihangir

🍸 18Mellow

🍸 Bars & wine● Alternative
Kılıçali Paşa Mah., Akarsu Yokuşu No:30/B, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 My go-to place in Cihangir for a drink, and maybe a quick bite, with friends after work.

💬 The Tinkerbell cocktail (passion fruit and kiwi) is delicious.

A two-floor tapas kitchen and bar on Akarsu Yokuşu, Cihangir's main bar street, for easy after-work drinks; the upstairs floor is the nicer one. It serves Turkish-fusion tapas and mezes with boutique Turkish wines and cocktails, and Time Out reviewed it in 2015.

🍸 25Solera Winery

🍸 Bars & wine● Trendy
Tomtom Mah., Yeni Çarşı Cd. No:44, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 A small bar with a huge variety of wines.

A cosy, crowded wine cave off İstiklal, lined floor-to-ceiling with bottles, with a huge choice of Turkish wines alongside imported ones. Bottles can be bought to take away at a discount, and the food (simple mezes, cheese and pasta) is secondary. It is a long-standing small shop and bar, and one of the pioneers of Istanbul's wine-bar scene.

🍽️ 105Viktor Levi Şarap Evi

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍸 bars● Classic
Caferağa Mh., Moda Cd., Damacı Sk. No:4, Kadıköy/İstanbul (behind Reks Cinema)

💬 A beautiful garden to enjoy a long evening dinner with friends (Moda).

A big, lively wine house in Moda with a large courtyard garden for long dinners with friends, where the wine matters more than the food. It pours house wines such as Papaz Karası and Ada Karası and fruit wines, with a bistro menu of grills, pasta and cheese plates. It descends from the historic Viktor Levi wine shop in Beyoğlu, reportedly founded in 1914; the Beyoğlu venue has closed.
