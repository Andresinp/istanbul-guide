Cihangir

🪴 11Journey

🪴 Cafés to work & hang outalso 🍳 breakfastalso 🍸 bars● Trendy
Kılıçali Paşa Mah., Akarsu Cd. (Akarsu Yokuşu) No:21/A, Cihangir, Beyoğlu/İstanbul
In practice: Laid-back all-day Cihangir cafe-restaurant with a work-friendly upstairs; good food, a bit pricey.

💬 Delicious food in a plant-filled space. Lots of freelancers work from here, especially on weekdays.

Two-storey café-restaurant on Akarsu Caddesi in Cihangir, on Foursquare since 2010 and a neighbourhood staple. Turkish-inspired food with a European twist (e.g. honey-roasted lamb shank), brunch, cocktails, and a mezzanine/upstairs with a communal table popular with laptop workers.

🪴 12Susam Cafe

🪴 Cafés to work & hang outalso 🍳 breakfastalso 🍸 bars● Trendy
Kılıçali Paşa Mah., Susam Sk. No:11, Cihangir, Beyoğlu/İstanbul
In practice: Relaxed all-day Cihangir cafe that turns into a bar at night; terrace tables.

💬 Great place to work if you want good food too. Has outside and semi-outside tables, a few with plug sockets.

Long-running neighbourhood café-bar on Susam Sokak in Cihangir, reviewed by Time Out back in 2011. Serves breakfast, salads, burgers, wraps, pasta and flatbread pizza, and stays open late as a bar; its signature drink is the 'Egeli Mojito' with black mulberry.

🪴 13Emily's Garden

🪴 Cafés to work & hang outalso 🍳 breakfastalso 🍸 bars● Trendy
Pürtelaş Hasan Efendi Mah., Susam Sk. No:13, 34427 Cihangir, Beyoğlu/İstanbul
In practice: Hidden garden bistro: calm daytime work/brunch spot, romantic dinner and drinks at night.

💬 A gorgeous garden in the middle of Cihangir. Quite fancy at night, but in the daytime it's quiet and peaceful.

Garden restaurant on Susam Sokak in Cihangir, on the site formerly occupied by The White Mill (which moved to Akaretler); on Foursquare since 2019. A small café front leads to a bar and long tables and a tree-lit back garden; Mediterranean/Italian-leaning menu with pasta and seafood, plus breakfast and brunch.

🍳 3Ağa Bilardo Cafe (the menemen billiard hall)

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)also 🪴 cafés● Alternative
Firuzağa, Taktaki Yokuşu No:2/A (off Sıraselviler Cd.), Cihangir, 34425 Beyoğlu/İstanbul
In practice: Old-school Cihangir billiard hall/kahvehane doing cheap tea, tost and (per Andrés) a great menemen.

💬 The best menemen, in a weird place: a billiard hall in Cihangir.

💬 Perfect for two: share a menemen with cheese and a gözleme with cheese, with tea.

Neighbourhood billiard hall and café in Cihangir where locals play billiards, okey and tavla. Reviewers praise the tost (with pickles on the side) and always-fresh tea, and one mekan.com reviewer calls it 'perhaps the only billiard hall you can come to with family'.

⚠ Identified as the only billiard hall found in Cihangir; no review mentions its menemen yet. Confirm it's the right one.

🍳 5Kahve 6

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)also 🪴 cafés● Trendy
Kılıçali Paşa Mah., Akarsu Cad., Anahtar Sk. No:13/A, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul
In practice: Cosy Cihangir breakfast café with a green back garden, Andrés's #1.

💬 Favourite #1. Gourmet, curated ingredients, back garden.

💬 Try the İzmir breakfast, the goat cheese omelette and the lor peynirli gözleme.

Long-standing Cihangir neighbourhood café (already reviewed by Time Out in 2011) with a back garden with a pool, open for breakfast and home cooking. Yemek.com (2024) highlights the 'Dolu Dolu' breakfast, the İzmir breakfast (sahanda Bergama tulum, Tire karadut, lor), the aubergine börek, the lor gözleme and the house granola.

🍽️ 10Limonlu Bahçe

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍳 breakfastalso 🪴 cafésalso 🍸 bars● Trendy₺₺
Tomtom Mh., Yeni Çarşı Cd. No:74, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul
💶 ₺₺ · ₺900–1,500 (a main ₺660–1,995 plus a drink) pp (research)
🍸 cocktail ₺650–760 (most cocktails) · 🍷 wine ₺470 (house Ancyra by the glass) · ☕ coffee ₺175 Turkish coffee / ₺260 latte source ↗
In practice: Leafy hidden garden for breakfast, a long lunch or evening drinks. More about the setting than the food.

💬 Turkish restaurant with very, very nice vibes in Cihangir, set in a garden that makes you forget the hustle and bustle of the city. My go-to place to bring people while it's still warm enough.

Opened in June 2001 and billed as Istanbul's first 'chill-out zone'. It is a 350 m² hidden garden of lemon, magnolia, pine and linden trees, with resident tortoises, behind the French Consulate on Yeni Çarşı. It serves breakfast through late drinks with a Turkish–Mediterranean menu, house lemonade and cocktails (official site).

Specialty coffee bar of Spada Roastery, in Teşvikiye since 2016. It is self-service with about 10 tables indoors and out, and serves espresso drinks, pour-overs of its own beans, Japanese matcha, house-made cold teas and refined-sugar-free bakes. Neighbourhood branch of Petra Roasting Co., one of Istanbul's early specialty roasters (HQ and roastery in Gayrettepe). Petra describes the Topağacı café as a real mahalle coffee house in historic Topağacı, with a 1930s-inspired interior, a bar-food menu and table service.

☕ 96Montag Coffee (Moda)

Caferağa Mah. Bademaltı Sok. No:22/A, Moda, Kadıköy/İstanbul
In practice: Local roaster's café on a Moda back street, steps from Story.

Moda branch of Montag Coffee, a Kadıköy specialty roaster that guides credit with bringing third-wave coffee to Kadıköy in the mid-2010s (2014–2015, sources differ). Montag roasts its own beans and has cafés in Kadıköy (Muvakkithane Cad.), Moda and Bomonti.
