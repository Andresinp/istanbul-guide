Bosphorus villages

🏘️ Neighbourhoods

🏘️ 87Arnavutköy

🏘️ Neighbourhoods● Classic

💬 A beautiful part of Istanbul on the water. Lovely to walk around. Get brunch at A Bit of Eggo, an afternoon beer at Hide Arnavutköy, and vegan food at Gabfoods. You can walk or scooter up along the water to Bebek and beyond, e.g. to Emirgan.

A pretty Bosphorus village of wooden Ottoman houses in Beşiktaş district, between Kuruçeşme and Bebek. A seafront walking and cycling path runs along the water north to Bebek and beyond, making it good for a stroll.

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)

🍳 88A Bit of Eggo

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)also ☀️ all-day● Classic
Abdülhak Molla Sk. No:26, Arnavutköy, 34345 Beşiktaş/İstanbul

💬 Brunch in Arnavutköy.

An egg-focused café on a quiet Arnavutköy back street, serving breakfast all day: menemen, frittata and an 'Egg'on Kruvasan' croissant with poached egg, plus layered crêpe desserts. There is a tree-shaded outdoor area.

✨ Fancy dinners

✨ 89MSA'nın Restoranı (Sakıp Sabancı Museum)

✨ Fancy dinnersalso 🍳 breakfast● Posh₺₺₺
Emirgan Mah., Sakıp Sabancı Cd. No:42, 34467 Sarıyer/İstanbul (inside Sakıp Sabancı Museum)
💶 ₺₺₺ · ₺2,500–4,000 for a main + drink (derived from menu) pp (research)
🍸 cocktail ₺950 · 🍷 wine ₺550–950 · 🍽️ plate ₺1,400–3,600 (mains) source ↗ source 2 ↗

A smart restaurant with a Bosphorus-view terrace in the Sakıp Sabancı Museum, run as the training restaurant of Mutfak Sanatları Akademisi (MSA, Istanbul Culinary Arts Academy): students cook and serve under professional chefs. The food is strong, mixing world cuisines with seasonal Turkish produce, though service can be patchy. It opened around 2017 in the space formerly occupied by Müzedechanga and is open to the public without a museum ticket.

🍸 Bars & wine

🍸 92Dirty Hands

🍸 Bars & winealso 🥙 street foodalso 🪩 clubs● Alternative
Akat, Zeytinoğlu Cd. No:7, 34335 Beşiktaş/İstanbul

A loud neighbourhood street-food bar in Akatlar serving fried kokoreç bites, bao, tacos, wraps and Tex-Mex burgers with cocktails and tequila shots. After 23:00 the tables are cleared and it turns into a small club. It comes from the team behind Arnavutköy's 'Any'.

🍸 Bars & winealso 🍳 breakfastalso ☀️ all-day● Trendy
Arnavutköy, Dere Sk. No:8, 34345 Beşiktaş/İstanbul

💬 An afternoon beer in Arnavutköy.

A café-restaurant in a large, leafy hidden garden courtyard off Arnavutköy's back streets, for brunch or an afternoon drink; pricier than its neighbours. The seasonal, French/American-leaning menu is built around breakfast and lunch, with bakery items, desserts, freshly ground coffee and vegan and gluten-free options.

🍸 94All Sports Cafe

🍸 Bars & winealso ☀️ all-day● Classic
Etiler, Bıyıklı Mehmet Paşa Sk. No:1 D:2, 34337 Beşiktaş/İstanbul

A long-running Etiler neighbourhood café-restaurant decorated with sports memorabilia, more café than bar, serving bowls and desserts. There is a fixed menu plus a rotating daily menu and a small summer garden. A regular says it has been around for 25 years, and there is also a Nişantaşı branch.

🍸 95Taps Bebek

🍸 Bars & wine● Classic
Bebek, Cevdetpaşa Cd. No:119, 34342 Beşiktaş/İstanbul

A relaxed, busy waterfront brewpub in a three-storey house on the Bosphorus shore, with Bosphorus and marina views. It brews its own beers (red ale, strong ale, kölsch, rotating drafts) and serves burgers and international pub food. Taps started in Nişantaşı in 2002 and has been in Bebek since 2008.

🏛️ Sights, museums & walks

🏛️ 96Sakıp Sabancı Museum (Emirgan)

🏛️ Sights, museums & walks● Must-see₺
Sakıp Sabancı Cd. No:42, Emirgan, 34467 Sarıyer/İstanbul
💶 ₺ · ₺450 adult ticket (₺330 discounted, ₺225 student; free on Tuesdays) pp (research)

💬 Beautiful museum gardens. The museum has a fixed collection as well as changing exhibitions.

Sabancı University's art museum in the 'Atlı Köşk', a former Sabancı family mansion on the Bosphorus in Emirgan, with terraced gardens overlooking the water. It holds a permanent collection of Ottoman calligraphy and paintings and hosts big temporary international exhibitions.
