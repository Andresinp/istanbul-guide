Cihangir

🏘️ Neighbourhoods

🏘️ 1Cihangir

🏘️ Neighbourhoods● Alternative

💬 Most of the places in this guide are in Cihangir or the wider Beyoğlu area.

A leafy, café-heavy residential quarter and the base for most places in this guide. It sits on a hillside in Beyoğlu between Taksim/Sıraselviler and the Bosphorus shore at Fındıklı, and is known for its bohemian, artsy reputation, its cafés and its street cats. Cihangir Mosque stands at its lower end.

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)

🍳 2Doğacıyız Gourmet

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)● Classic₺
Kılıçali Paşa, Akarsu Yokuşu Sk. No:41A, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul
💶 ₺ · ₺725 (set breakfast) plus drinks; à la carte menemen ₺220–300 pp (research)
🍽️ plate ₺220–395 (menemen/gözleme/omelettes) source ↗

💬 Eastern cuisine, more touristy.

A Hatay-flavoured breakfast house in Cihangir, different from the usual Turkish kahvaltı and more geared to visitors. It is known for the serpme Antakya breakfast (biberli ekmek, tuzlu yoğurt, zahter, muhammara, sürk), muhlama and Hatay-style gözleme. By its own account it has been running for about 14 years, and Yemek.com says its produce comes from its own land and is sold by the jar.

🍳 3Ağa Bilardo Cafe (the menemen billiard hall)

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)also ☀️ all-day● Alternative
Firuzağa, Taktaki Yokuşu No:2/A (off Sıraselviler Cd.), Cihangir, 34425 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 The best menemen, in a weird place: a billiard hall in Cihangir.

💬 Perfect for two: share a menemen with cheese and a gözleme with cheese, with tea.

An old-school Cihangir billiard hall and kahvehane where locals play billiards, okey and tavla over cheap, always-fresh tea. Reviewers praise the tost, served with pickles on the side. One reviewer called it perhaps the only billiard hall you can come to with family.

🍳 4Van Kahvaltı Evi

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)● Classic
Kılıçali Paşa / Firuzağa, Defterdar Yokuşu No:52/A, 34425 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Favourite #2. Simple, less sophisticated, but everything you get is delicious.

A hugely popular Cihangir institution serving Van-style (eastern Anatolian) breakfast: murtuğa, kavut, otlu peynir, çemen, pişi, gözleme and fresh pomegranate juice. It is famous for its weekend queue; weekdays are calmer. There is a sister branch in Nişantaşı (Valikonağı Cad., Sezai Selek Sk. 19).

🍳 5Kahve 6

🍳 Breakfast (kahvaltı)also ☀️ all-day● Trendy
Kılıçali Paşa Mah., Akarsu Cad., Anahtar Sk. No:13/A, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Favourite #1. Gourmet, curated ingredients, back garden.

💬 Try the İzmir breakfast, the goat cheese omelette and the lor peynirli gözleme.

A cosy, long-standing Cihangir breakfast café with a green back garden with a pool, serving breakfast and home cooking. Highlights include the 'Dolu Dolu' breakfast, the İzmir breakfast (sahanda Bergama tulum, Tire karadut, lor), the aubergine börek, the lor gözleme and the house granola. Time Out was already reviewing it in 2011.

🍲 Lokantas: home cooking

🍲 9Fıccın Lokanta Cihangir

🍲 Lokantas: home cooking● Classic
Akarsu Yokuşu 44/A, Firuzağa, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Andrés: definitely.

A Cihangir lokanta serving Circassian and home-style dishes: Circassian velibah (a gözleme-like pastry) and Circassian mantı alongside mücver, Kayseri yağlaması and zeytinyağlılar. It is the second branch of Fıccın on Kallavi Sokak in Asmalımescit, opened on Firuzağa Square in 2026.

🍲 10Mayko Lokanta

🍲 Lokantas: home cooking● Classic₺₺
Kuloğlu Mah., Hacıoğlu Sk. No:1A, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul
💶 ₺₺ · ₺1,000–1,300 with drinks pp (research)
🍽️ plate ₺200–850 source ↗

A tiny, cosy Cihangir restaurant with a short menu of modern home-style Turkish dishes and meze, including dolma, eggplant balls, fava and many vegan options, which makes it good for mixed vegan and meat-eating groups. 🍽️ 12Limonlu Bahçe

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🍳 breakfastalso ☀️ all-dayalso 🍸 bars● Trendy₺₺
Tomtom Mh., Yeni Çarşı Cd. No:74, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul
💶 ₺₺ · ₺900–1,500 (a main ₺660–1,995 plus a drink) pp (research)
🍸 cocktail ₺650–760 (most cocktails) · 🍷 wine ₺470 (house Ancyra by the glass) · ☕ coffee ₺175 Turkish coffee / ₺260 latte source ↗

💬 Turkish restaurant with very, very nice vibes in Cihangir, set in a garden that makes you forget the hustle and bustle of the city. My go-to place to bring people while it's still warm enough.

A leafy hidden garden for breakfast, a long lunch or evening drinks, more about the setting than the food. The 350 m² garden of lemon, magnolia, pine and linden trees, with resident tortoises, sits behind the French Consulate on Yeni Çarşı, and the Turkish–Mediterranean menu runs from breakfast to late drinks, with house lemonade and cocktails. It opened in June 2001, billed as Istanbul's first 'chill-out zone'.

☀️ All-day food & drinks

☀️ 13Journey

☀️ All-day food & drinksalso 🍳 breakfastalso 🍸 bars● Trendy
Kılıçali Paşa Mah., Akarsu Cd. (Akarsu Yokuşu) No:21/A, Cihangir, Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Delicious food in a plant-filled space. Lots of freelancers work from here, especially on weekdays.

A laid-back, two-storey all-day café-restaurant on Akarsu Caddesi, with a work-friendly upstairs mezzanine whose communal table is popular with laptop workers. The food is Turkish-inspired with a European twist (such as honey-roasted lamb shank), plus brunch and cocktails; good, if a bit pricey. It has been a neighbourhood staple since at least 2010.

Pürtelaş Hasan Efendi Mah., Susam Sk. No:13, 34427 Cihangir, Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 A gorgeous garden in the middle of Cihangir. Quite fancy at night, but in the daytime it's quiet and peaceful.

A hidden garden bistro on Susam Sokak: a calm daytime spot for work or brunch, and romantic dinners and drinks at night. 🍸 25Solera Winery

🍸 Bars & wine● Trendy
Tomtom Mah., Yeni Çarşı Cd. No:44, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 A small bar with a huge variety of wines.

A cosy, crowded wine cave off İstiklal, lined floor-to-ceiling with bottles, with a huge choice of Turkish wines alongside imported ones. Bottles can be bought to take away at a discount, and the food (simple mezes, cheese and pasta) is secondary. It is a long-standing small shop and bar, and one of the pioneers of Istanbul's wine-bar scene.

🪩 Clubs / electronic music

🪩 27MiniMüzikhol

🪩 Clubs / electronic music● Alternative
Soğancı Sk. (Cihangir Palas) No:3/1, Cihangir, Beyoğlu/İstanbul

An intimate, sweaty underground room for house and minimal, and the late-night reference for Cihangir. The small, unsigned club (capacity 200) is on the ground floor of a Cihangir apartment block and has been running since around 2009–2010. It bills itself as the oldest underground club in Istanbul and has hosted DJs such as Ivan Smagghe, Acid Pauli and DJ Tennis.

🛁 Hamams & treat yourself

🛁 30Let's Relax Cihangir

🛁 Hamams & treat yourself● Classic₺₺₺
Kuloğlu Mah., Sıraselviler Cd., Mavi Plaza No:84 Kat:2, Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 45-minute massage: Thai, Thai with oil ('Thai for beginners') or a standard Swedish.

A clean, professional Thai-style massage studio, rated 5.0 from 112 reviews on Fresha. Thai therapists offer Thai, Thai-with-oil, Swedish, aromatherapy and deep-tissue massages, plus nails and waxing. It is the Cihangir branch of a small Istanbul chain (Taksim İstiklal, Cihangir, Beşiktaş, Nişantaşı), apparently unrelated to the Thai 'Let's Relax Spa' chain.

🛁 31Ağa Hamamı

🛁 Hamams & treat yourself● Classic₺₺₺
Turnacıbaşı Sk., Kuloğlu, Beyoğlu/İstanbul (OSM: No:66; Time Out: No:48)
💶 ₺₺₺ · ₺3,100–6,200 pp (research)

💬 Around 100 m down the road. Lovely, though a little more touristy. Most hamams are single-sex; Ağa Hamamı is mixed.

A small historic hamam. The traditional package is ₺3,600 by card or ₺3,100 cash (entry ₺1,800, kese scrub ₺900, foam wash ₺900), or ₺5,600 / ₺5,100 with a 20-minute oil massage; kese, soap, shampoo, peştemal, towel and slippers are provided. The hamam says Mehmed the Conqueror built it in 1454 as a hunting lodge with a bath below, renovated in 1844 under Abdülmecid, and markets itself as Istanbul's oldest Turkish bath.
