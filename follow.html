How the scene works

  • Resident Advisor has the most complete Istanbul club calendar: about 45 RA listings for 8 Oct to 5 Nov 2026. The big Klein nights and the hard-techno venues are often listed only on Bugece, so check both. ↗
  • Bugece (bugece.co) is the main ticket platform for Istanbul club nights. Advance tickets mostly cost about 400 to 1,100 TL, and headliners cost more (e.g. Cloudy at 2,290 TL on RA). Buy ahead for big names, because door sales are not guaranteed. ↗
  • The door decides. Klein's published rules say staff judge attitude, manner, clothing and general suitability and may consider gender balance, and the decision is final. Some guides warn that all-male groups are often refused even with tickets. ↗
  • Clubs are 18+ and check ID at the door. Foreign visitors should carry a passport or a copy. ↗
  • Go late. Beyoğlu clubs rarely fill before 01:00. Most RA listings start at 22:00 to 23:00 and run to 04:00 to 06:00. ↗
  • Use taxi apps (BiTaksi, Uber) late at night. Klein Phönix and Flux are out in Maslak, near M2 Atatürk Oto Sanayi, so plan the ride home. The Beyoğlu clubs (Frankhan, Kastel, Minimüzikhol, Under, Şahika, Suma Han) are within walking distance of each other. ↗
  • 28 Oct is Republic Day eve (the 29th is a holiday), so there are midweek parties that night: Mahmut Orhan at Klein Phönix, DØMINA at 74HALL, and Halloween parties at Klein Harbiye and Frankhan. ↗
  • Shops/markets (incl. tekel/bakkal) may sell alcohol only 06:00–22:00 (2013 law); there is no such time limit in bars and restaurants. Buy bottles before 22:00. ↗
  • Weekend night metro: Friday 06:00 to Sunday 00:00 non-stop on M2 (Şişhane/Taksim–Hacıosman/Yenikapı), M4 (Kadıköy), M1A/B, M5, M6, M7; from 00:30 to 05:30 each tap costs a double fare. ↗
  • Şehir Hatları runs a Kadıköy–Karaköy–Beşiktaş late service only on Friday and Saturday nights; otherwise ferry frequency drops in the evening, so check the last boat back before crossing. ↗
  • Istanbulkart works on metro, tram, funiculars, Marmaray, buses, Metrobüs and city ferries; buy it from machines at metro entrances (non-refundable deposit) and top up at kiosks. Up to five people can travel on one card — tap, wait a few seconds, tap again. ↗
  • Taxis: insist on the meter (drivers sometimes push a fixed price); no night surcharge; tipping is generally unnecessary. Taxis can be hailed via apps (Uber, Bolt). ↗
  • Football tickets go through Passo and need the Passo Taraftar Kart (renamed Passolig; existing Passolig cards still work). The official page doesn't spell out foreigner rules — check application requirements in advance; derby tickets need booking well ahead. ↗
  • Indoor smoking has been banned in bars, restaurants and cafés since July 2009, but enforcement is patchy; many venues offer heated terraces for smokers. ↗
  • Republic Day: holiday from 13:00 Wed 28 Oct and all of Thu 29 Oct — banks, notaries and public offices closed; Fri 30 Oct is a normal working day. ↗
  • Ticketing is split by organiser. Akbank Caz Festivali is on Biletix. Filmekimi and Fiba Salon İKSV are on Passo (website, app or outlets), with İKSV Lale Kart members getting presale. Foreign-card and Turkish ID problems could not be confirmed from Biletix or Passo help pages, which did not load. ↗
  • Start times run late: opera, ballet and theatre at 20:00, Zorlu PSM jazz at 20:30, club gigs at Babylon at 21:30 and late sets around 22:00. ↗
  • Pera Museum is closed Mondays; entry is free every Friday 18:00-22:00 and for students on Wednesdays. ↗
  • Free art: Salt (Beyoğlu and Galata, Tue-Sat 11-19, Sun 11-18), Arter's 'Work in Progress' and the Artweeks Collector's Edition at Akaretler all cost nothing. Borusan Contemporary is open weekends only (10-19). ↗
  • The Museum Pass Istanbul (about 6,500 TL as of April 2026, 5 days from first entry) covers state museums like Topkapı (closed Tuesdays) and the Archaeological Museums, but not private art museums such as Istanbul Modern, Pera or Sakıp Sabancı. ↗
