Cihangir

🥙 6Cihangir Çiğ Köfte

🥙 Street food & cheap eats● Alternative
Turnacıbaşı Cd. 61a, Katip Mustafa Çelebi, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Get the çiğ köfte dürüm.

A hole-in-the-wall takeaway on Turnacıbaşı Caddesi selling çiğ köfte, the meatless bulgur-and-pepper-paste version, in dürüm or by the portion. A cheap vegan snack.

🥙 7Halil Usta Kebap & Lahmacun

🥙 Street food & cheap eatsalso 🍽️ turkish● Classic
Katip Mustafa Çelebi Mah., Hocazade Sk. No:7/A (off Sıraselviler Cd.), 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

🥙 Street food & cheap eatsalso 🍲 lokantasalso 🍽️ turkish● Classic₺

💶 ₺ · ₺400–800 pp (research)
🍽️ plate ₺230–750 source ↗

💬 For Adana kebab, lahmacun, lentil soup and the tavuk (chicken) wrap.

A sit-down Diyarbakır-style stone-oven lahmacun and kebab restaurant on Turnacıbaşı Caddesi, between Galatasaray and Cihangir. The menu covers Diyarbakır and Antep lahmacun, pide, Adana and Urfa kebab, sac tava, lentil soup, dürüms including chicken şiş, and künefe, plus breakfast.

Beyoğlu, Taksim & Galata

🥙 34Suat Usta Mersin Tantuni

🥙 Street food & cheap eats● Classic
Katip Mustafa Çelebi Mah., Büyükparmakkapı Tel Sk. No:1 (Çukurlu Çeşme Sk. corner), 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

A long-running, late-night Mersin tantuni counter in the back streets off İstiklal near Taksim, and the obvious replacement for the closed Emine Ana. It is a one-dish shop serving tantuni dürüm with Mersin şalgam and pickled peppers, where crowds from İstiklal and Nevizade end their night.

🥙 35Anas Chicken

🥙 Street food & cheap eatsalso 🌍 international● Classic
Kuloğlu Mah., Sadri Alışık Sk. No:8, 34433 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 A chain with restaurants mostly in Syria and a few in Turkey; Syrian friends are obsessed with it. Get the fried chicken or a dürüm to go for an easy lunch.

Syrian fried chicken and shawarma, mainly takeaway: broasted chicken, chicken shawarma dürüm and kabsa. The chain (Anas Gıda Grubu) was founded in Damascus in 1991 and lists three active Istanbul branches: Beyoğlu (Taksim), a short walk from İstiklal, plus Aksaray and Kayaşehir.

🥙 36Dürümzade

🥙 Street food & cheap eatsalso 🍽️ turkish● Classic
Hüseyinağa Mah., Kamer Hatun Cd. No:26/A (corner of Topçekenler Sk.), 34435 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Hole-in-the-wall for Hatay food, spicier than western Turkey's. Famous since Anthony Bourdain gave it his blessing. The best dürüm I've had in Turkey.

A tiny hole-in-the-wall charcoal dürüm shop a few minutes' walk from İstiklal Caddesi, serving lamb, Adana, chicken and liver dürüm. It became famous after Anthony Bourdain ate a lamb şiş dürüm there for the Istanbul episode of 'No Reservations', and has had a lot of tourist traffic since. The double-lamb 'duble kuzu dürüm' is the signature.

Karaköy & Tophane

🥙 61Karaköy Çorba Evi

🥙 Street food & cheap eatsalso 🍲 lokantas● Classic
Kemankeş Karamustafa Paşa Mah., Mumhane Cd. No:35/A, 34425 Beyoğlu/İstanbul

💬 Soups. You can ask for a ½ portion as a first course, then go on to the fish restaurant.

A cheap soup house on Mumhane Caddesi with about 20 soups, from mercimek and ezogelin to işkembe and kelle paça; order a half portion as a starter before a fish dinner. It started in 2011 as a soup-and-stew place and has traded as Karaköy Çorba Evi since 2014. Its website also mentions a Kadıköy location.

🍽️ 11Tomtom Kebap

🍽️ Kebab, meyhane & Turkish restaurantsalso 🥙 street food● Classic₺
Firuzağa, Yeni Çarşı Cd. 39 A, 34425 Beyoğlu/İstanbul
💶 ₺ · ~₺600 pp (research)

A tiny kebab and dürüm shop on Yeni Çarşı Caddesi, very popular with tourists and often with a queue. It is known for Adana and Urfa kebab, lamb dürüm, hummus and lentil soup, with a few sidewalk tables. It is said to have been founded in 2018.

🍸 82Dekk

🍸 Bars & winealso 🥙 street food● Trendy
Harbiye, Darülbedai Cd. No:4 (Lütfi Kırdar Congress Centre terrace), 34367 Şişli/İstanbul

An open-air 2,500 m² food hall and cocktail bar (dekkBar) on the terrace of the Lütfi Kırdar Congress Centre near Maçka Park, with street-food stalls, concerts and workshops; best in good weather. It opened in autumn 2021, and a second, indoor-outdoor dekk opened in Akmerkez on 11 October 2025.

⚠ Dekk's own site now lists only its Akmerkez location (opened Oct 2025); this Maçka terrace may have closed. Check before going.

🍸 96Dirty Hands

🍸 Bars & winealso 🥙 street foodalso 🪩 clubs● Alternative
Akat, Zeytinoğlu Cd. No:7, 34335 Beşiktaş/İstanbul

A loud neighbourhood street-food bar in Akatlar serving fried kokoreç bites, bao, tacos, wraps and Tex-Mex burgers with cocktails and tequila shots. After 23:00 the tables are cleared and it turns into a small club. It comes from the team behind Arnavutköy's 'Any'.
